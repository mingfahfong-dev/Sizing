Results (%)

When a value changes, its cell is highlighted, and two rows appear underneath: the pasted CCLAS values and the change. Hover over a highlighted cell to see both. Sub-sample rows are hidden here. They still feed the composite, and you can edit them in the sieve masses table. On composite rows, +9.5 and +6.3 add the 2 dp % values, like your sheet formulas. On other rows they add the unrounded values, like CCLAS. −9.5 mm is calculated as 100 − (+9.5 mm). The lower block's formula (=100−AF50) looks like a slip, so it isn't used here.
